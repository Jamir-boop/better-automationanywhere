## Platform

web

## Users

Automation Anywhere developers and operators who work in A360 Control Rooms and need faster, clearer development workflows.

## Product Purpose

Better AA Developer Experience adds developer tools, interface improvements, Control Room utilities, and Automation Anywhere workflow helpers. Success means users complete common development tasks with less friction while keeping explicit control over the target Control Room and page.

## Positioning

The extension combines in-page Automation Anywhere improvements with Control Room-aware tools, portable clipboard workflows, and a local Better Recorder bridge in one browser extension.

## Operating Context

Users run the extension in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox beside authenticated Automation Anywhere Control Room pages. The side panel is the primary tool surface, and a full options page provides shared Appearance, Settings, and Help views.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Support Chrome, Edge, and Firefox without adding telemetry.
- Keep settings global across Control Rooms and preserve explicit target selection for Tools.
- Preserve English and Spanish extension interfaces.
- Keep live diagnostics read-only and contextual to the side panel.
